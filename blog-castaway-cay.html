Everything You Need to Know About Disney Castaway Cay

February 2025 · 6 min read · By Gather & Go Travel Co.

← Back to Tips & Guides

If you've ever looked into a Disney cruise, you've probably heard about Disney Castaway Cay — Disney's private island in the Bahamas that serves as a port stop on most Caribbean and Bahamas sailings. And if you've heard anything about it, you've probably heard that it's incredible.

That reputation is earned. Disney Castaway Cay is, in my opinion, one of the best beach experiences available anywhere — and the fact that it's exclusive to Disney Cruise Line guests is one of the biggest advantages of booking a Disney cruise over any other line.

Here's everything your family needs to know before you go.

What Makes Disney Castaway Cay Different

The most immediately obvious thing about Disney Castaway Cay is how it feels compared to typical cruise port stops. On most cruises, when you dock at a Caribbean island, you disembark into a port full of shops and taxis and people trying to sell you excursions. It can feel chaotic and commercial.

Disney Castaway Cay is different. Disney owns the entire island, which means they control everything about it. The ship docks directly at the island — there's no tender boat, you literally walk off the gangway and you're on the island within minutes. The environment is beautiful and well-maintained. The staff are Disney cast members. The beach is clean, the water is crystal clear, and the whole place has that unmistakable Disney attention to detail.

There are no lines of taxis or aggressive vendors. It's just a beautiful Bahamian island, completely set up for your family to have a spectacular beach day.

The Beaches

Disney Castaway Cay has separate beaches for families and adults, which is a genuinely thoughtful design decision.

Family Beach

This is where you'll spend most of your time if you have kids. The beach is long, beautiful, and backed by palm trees and scattered Disney details. The water is warm, calm, and crystal clear — ideal for little ones. There are floats and inflatables for kids, a splash pad area for younger children, and beach chairs as far as the eye can see. The snorkeling lagoon is a highlight — you can rent equipment and explore an underwater trail that includes a sunken plane, a fake treasure chest, and various fish. It's genuinely magical even for older kids and adults.

Serenity Bay — Adults-Only Beach

A tram ride away from the family beach, Serenity Bay is a quieter, adults-only stretch of beach with its own bar, lounge chairs, and a more peaceful atmosphere. If you're traveling with older teens who can manage themselves, or if you want a couple of hours away from the kids while they're in the Scuttle's Cove kids club on the island, this is a lovely option.

Activities on the Island

There is no shortage of things to do on Disney Castaway Cay, from the completely free to the add-on extras:

  • Snorkeling — equipment rentals are available, and the snorkeling lagoon is excellent. This is a must-do if anyone in your family enjoys snorkeling.
  • Bike rentals — you can rent bikes and ride around the island. It's a great way to explore and get some exercise in between beach sessions.
  • Water sports — kayaks, paddleboats, banana boat rides, and tube rentals are all available for an additional cost.
  • Scuttle's Cove — a kids' activity area on the island with games, supervised activities, and a play area. Great if you want to give the adults some Serenity Bay time.
  • 5K run — Disney holds a Disney Castaway Cay 5K race on the island for guests who want to participate. You get a medal. It's genuinely popular.
  • Shopping — there are a handful of shops on the island with Disney Castaway Cay-exclusive merchandise you can't get anywhere else, including some items with the island's own branding.

Food & Drinks

There are multiple dining pavilions on Disney Castaway Cay, and the food is included in your cruise fare — no extra charge for the basic food options. The BBQ buffet is typically excellent: grilled burgers, hot dogs, chicken, fish, salads, sides, and desserts. It's beachside dining done right, and the quality is noticeably better than you might expect from a buffet on a Caribbean island.

There's also a dedicated bar, Cookie's BBQ, and a ice cream stand. Alcoholic drinks are available for purchase. Frozen drinks on a beach chair on a warm Bahamian afternoon — you don't need me to talk you into that.

Bring a Refillable Mug

If you purchased a refillable mug package on the ship, your mug works on Disney Castaway Cay too. Free soft drinks all day on the island? Yes, please.

Tips to Make the Most of Your Disney Castaway Cay Day

Get off the ship as early as possible. The beach chairs closest to the water go fast. Disney cast members start allowing guests off the ship the moment they're cleared — usually well before the official start time. Be ready to walk off the moment the gangway opens.

Claim your chairs first, then go explore. Put your stuff down, get your spot secured, and then head off to rent equipment, grab breakfast, or explore. Don't wander first and then try to find chairs — the good ones are gone.

Rent snorkeling equipment early. The snorkeling lagoon is best in the morning when the water is clearest and there are fewer people. Equipment rentals are available near the family beach.

Leave time to go back to the ship. The ship's pools, restaurants, and shops are essentially empty while everyone is on the island. If you want to swim without a crowd or get a quiet lunch, spending an hour back on the ship in the early afternoon is a surprisingly good move.

Don't wait too long to eat. The BBQ pavilions can get busy around noon. Either eat early (11am) or wait until 1pm when the rush has died down.

The Bottom Line

Disney Castaway Cay is genuinely one of the best things about a Disney cruise, and for many families it ends up being their favorite day of the entire sailing. The combination of a beautiful beach, excellent food, Disney-quality service, and the ability to walk straight off the ship onto the island makes it a uniquely special experience. If you're on the fence about a Disney cruise, Disney Castaway Cay alone is worth serious consideration.
