The Best Character Dining at Walt Disney World® Resort (Ranked)

March 2025 · 7 min read · By Gather & Go Travel Co.

← Back to Tips & Guides

Character dining at Walt Disney World® Resort isn't just a meal. For a lot of kids — and honestly, for a lot of adults — it's one of the most memorable parts of the entire trip. Sitting down to breakfast while Cinderella stops by your table, or watching your four-year-old completely freeze up when Mickey Mouse walks toward them and then absolutely lose it with excitement? Those are the moments that make the whole vacation.

But not all character dining is created equal. Some experiences are worth moving mountains to get a reservation. Others are fine but not worth the premium price. I've helped enough families navigate this that I have strong opinions, and I'm going to share them.

Important Booking Note

Character dining reservations open 60 days before your check-in date. The most popular experiences — especially Cinderella's Royal Table — book up within hours of that window opening. If these are on your must-do list, you need a plan for booking them the moment that window opens.

The Rankings

#1
Hard to Get Must Do

Cinderella's Royal Table

Magic Kingdom® Park · Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner · Inside the Castle

This one is in a category of its own, and not just because of the food. You eat inside Cinderella's Castle. Actual inside the castle, looking out at the park through leaded glass windows. Cinderella herself greets you at the top of the spiral staircase before you're seated, and other princesses (Aurora, Ariel, Snow White) visit your table throughout the meal.

The food is good — not transcendent, but good — but that's almost beside the point. The setting is unmatched anywhere on Disney property. If you have a child who is into Disney princesses even a little bit, this experience is worth every penny and every effort to secure a reservation. I've seen grown adults tear up at this meal, and I completely understand why.

#2
Hard to Get Must Do

Topolino's Terrace — Flavors of the Riviera

Disney's Riviera Resort · Breakfast Only · Rooftop Setting

This one surprised a lot of people when it opened, and it's now one of the most beloved character meals on property. Breakfast on the rooftop of the Riviera Resort, with sweeping views of EPCOT and Hollywood Studios, while Mickey, Minnie, Donald, and Daisy visit your table dressed in their French Riviera finest. The food is genuinely excellent — think pastries, French toast, egg dishes, beautiful presentation.

What sets this apart from most character meals is the combination of spectacular food, a stunning setting, and characters that actually take their time with each table. If you're staying at the Riviera (or are willing to take a Skyliner ride over), this breakfast is an absolute top-tier experience.

#3
Must Do

'Ohana Best Friends Breakfast

Disney's Polynesian Village Resort · Breakfast Only

Lilo and Stitch, Pluto, and Mickey welcome you at this fan-favorite breakfast in the Polynesian Resort's 'Ohana restaurant. The food is served family style — Mickey waffles, scrambled eggs, sausage, fresh fruit — and the characters are known for being particularly warm and interactive with kids. The atmosphere is relaxed and fun, the Polynesian resort itself is beautiful, and if you're lucky you can spot Magic Kingdom's fireworks through the windows on certain nights.

This one books up almost as fast as Cinderella's Royal Table, which tells you everything you need to know about how beloved it is.

#4
Must Do

Garden Grill Restaurant

EPCOT® · Lunch & Dinner

This one is criminally underrated. Garden Grill is a rotating restaurant in EPCOT's Land pavilion — it slowly revolves as you eat, giving you views into Living with the Land (the boat ride below). Mickey, Pluto, Chip, and Dale visit in their farmer outfits, and the food is farm-fresh and genuinely delicious. Skillet-cooked meats, vegetables, cornbread, and desserts, all family style.

It's easier to get a reservation than the top three spots, the food is arguably better than most character meals, and the atmosphere is genuinely charming. If you're spending a day at EPCOT, this is the one to book.

#5

Chef Mickey's

Disney's Contemporary Resort · Breakfast & Dinner

Chef Mickey's is the classic — Mickey, Minnie, Donald, Goofy, and Pluto in chef hats, huge buffet, the Contemporary Resort's iconic atrium. It's loud, it's busy, and it's exactly what you expect from a Disney character meal in the best possible way. The energy in that room is something else — kids absolutely love it, and it's a great option if you want a guaranteed Mickey experience without the pressure of a more coveted reservation.

It's not the quietest or the most refined meal on this list, but it delivers on the character interaction and the fun factor every single time.

#6
More Accessible

Akershus Royal Banquet Hall

EPCOT® · Norway Pavilion · Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

If you want the princess experience but can't get into Cinderella's Royal Table, Akershus is your answer. You meet a rotating lineup of Disney princesses — often Ariel, Belle, Snow White, Aurora, and Jasmine — in a beautiful Norwegian castle-style setting in EPCOT's World Showcase. The food is a Norwegian-inspired buffet that's solid if not spectacular.

Reservations are easier to get here than at Cinderella's Royal Table, and some kids actually prefer it because they get to meet more princesses. It's a genuinely worthwhile experience, and a smart backup plan if the castle books up before you can snag a reservation.

A Few Honorable Mentions

Hollywood & Vine at Disney's Hollywood Studios® has seasonal themed meals featuring characters like Minnie and friends in special outfits, and it's often easier to book than the top choices. Cape May Cafe at the Beach Club has a fun breakfast with Minnie and friends in beachwear. Tusker House at Disney's Animal Kingdom® Theme Park features Donald, Daisy, Mickey, and Goofy in safari gear — kids who are into the animals and nature themes really enjoy this one.

Tips for Getting the Reservations You Want

  • Know your 60-day window. Reservations open at 7am Eastern Time, 60 days before your check-in date. Be on the app or website at 6:55am ready to go.
  • Be flexible on meal time. If dinner at Cinderella's Royal Table isn't available, check breakfast. An early lunch at 11am is often easier to grab than a prime dinner slot.
  • Check for cancellations. Reservations open back up constantly as people modify or cancel their plans. If you didn't get what you wanted at 60 days, keep checking — especially in the week before your trip.
  • Book through a travel agent. This is genuinely one of the biggest advantages of using a Disney travel agent. We're ready with multiple devices at 7am on your 60-day mark, actively trying to get the exact reservations you want.
